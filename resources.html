Related resources

Seminars and events

  • Post-Bayes seminar series Online seminar series on post-Bayesian methods, organised in themed chapters. Due to resume in early 2027.
  • Post-Bayes YouTube channel Recorded seminar talks and lectures on selected topics in post-Bayesian inference.
  • Post-Bayes on the Beach (Newcastle, 8 December 2026) One-day course by Chris Oates at the Dove Marine Laboratory, Cullercoats: a tutorial on post-Bayesian methods, predictively oriented posteriors, and computation for post-Bayes.
  • OPTIMAL: Optimisation and Post-Bayesian Inference in Machine Learning (AISTATS 2026) Workshop connecting post-Bayesian inference with optimisation-based views of uncertainty and decision-making.
  • Advances in post-Bayesian methods (UCL, 2025) In-person workshop held at UCL, linked to the seminar series.

Software

  • edfong/MP Code accompanying Martingale posterior distributions, including copula-based predictive resampling.
  • jeremiasknoblauch/GVIPublic Code for generalised variational inference experiments.
  • pymc-devs/pymc-prop PyMC implementation of predictively oriented posteriors.
