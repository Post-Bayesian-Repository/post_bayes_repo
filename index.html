Post-Bayesian Repository

A curated list of papers on post-Bayesian inference: methods that, like Bayesian inference, produce a distribution over parameters or predictions, but use an update rule other than Bayes' rule. They are typically motivated by misspecified models or priors, posteriors that are costly to compute, or a focus on prediction. The list covers work from 2016 onwards; earlier precedents are listed on the About page.

Papers are tagged by topic and contribution style. Search by title, author or venue, use the filters to narrow the list, and click a column header to sort. Clicking a tag filters the table by it. New to the area? Start with the Background page.
